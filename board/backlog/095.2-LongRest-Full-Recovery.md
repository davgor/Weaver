# 095.2 — longRest recovers HP and clears rest-clearable state

Extend `longRest` so it remains the only full-recovery source: advance day by 1 and restore each listed character’s stats.

**Parent:** `095-CharacterEngine-Rest-Recovery-Api`. **Depends on:** `095.1`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `longRest({ campaignId, characterIds })` advances campaign day by exactly 1
- [ ] Each listed character with persisted stats ends at `currentHp === maxHp`, `dying === null`, and without rest-clearable conditions
- [ ] Characters without stats are skipped (no throw) so travel-only campaigns still advance the day
- [ ] Empty/omitted `characterIds` still advances the day (backward compatible with today’s day-only call sites) and recovers nobody
- [ ] Unit tests cover damaged / dying / conditioned PCs before and after rest
